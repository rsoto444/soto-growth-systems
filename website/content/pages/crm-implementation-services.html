Why the Last Step Matters Most

Most CRM implementations are judged at go-live. The leaks show up weeks later.

From our own audit

When we audited our own booking flow, we found the calendar's default consent checkbox was forced as required. Anyone who skipped it got an error and couldn't book a strategy call. It's exactly the kind of silent leak we look for in client systems: nothing looks broken, but qualified people drop off at the last step.

That is why every reconnected form, number and calendar is tested end to end before go-live. If your CRM already exists but nobody trusts it, see CRM consulting services instead.

CRM Implementation Questions

What is a CRM implementation?

CRM implementation is the process of setting up a new CRM for a business: designing the pipeline, moving data in, connecting forms, phone numbers and calendars, building follow-up and dashboards, and training the team. It is finished when the team works in the CRM every day, not when the software is switched on.
