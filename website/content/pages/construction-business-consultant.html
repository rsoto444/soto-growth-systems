The Six Growth Leaks We See in Contractor Businesses

Most contractors do not have a lead problem first. They have a follow-through problem. These are the six places the jobs slip away.

From our own audit

When we audited our own booking flow, we found the calendar's default consent checkbox was forced as required. Anyone who skipped it got an error and couldn't book a strategy call. It's exactly the kind of silent leak we look for in client systems: nothing looks broken, but qualified people drop off at the last step.

1. Slow reply to new enquiries

A homeowner with a broken AC unit or a leaking roof calls three companies. The one that calls back first usually gets the visit. When enquiries sit in a voicemail box or an inbox until the end of the day, the job often goes to whoever answered sooner.

2. Estimates sent and never followed up

The estimate goes out, the crew moves on to the next job, and nobody calls back. Big-ticket work like a new system, a solar install, a roof replacement or a driveway pour rarely gets decided on the first visit. Without a set follow-up sequence, those estimates go quiet on their own.

3. A CRM nobody uses, or no CRM at all

Leads live in phones, notebooks, text threads and the owner's memory. Or the business paid for a CRM that the team works around instead of in. Either way, nobody can see which leads are open, which estimates are pending and who owns the next step.

4. No clear sales process

Every estimator or salesperson sells a different way: different questions, different pricing conversations, different follow-up. When results depend on who took the call, the business cannot train new people or tell what is actually working.

5. No weekly numbers

The owner knows how busy the crews are, but not the close rate, the average job value, the number of open estimates or how fast leads get a reply. Without those numbers, every decision about hiring, pricing or marketing spend is a guess.

6. Everything runs through the owner

Pricing, approvals, callbacks and scheduling all wait for the owner. The business can only grow as fast as one person can answer the phone, and the owner cannot step away from the trucks long enough to fix the system.

HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Roofing, Solar and Concrete

The six leaks look a little different in each trade. The fix is the same system, set up for how your jobs are sold.

HVAC business consultant

HVAC runs on two speeds: urgent repair calls that go to whoever answers first, and replacement estimates that take days or weeks to decide. We set up fast replies for the first and a follow-up sequence for the second, and track both in one pipeline.

Plumbing business consultant

Plumbing calls are often urgent, so reply speed decides most jobs. Larger work like repipes and water heater replacements needs a quote follow-up the same way any big-ticket estimate does. We build both into the system.

Electrical contractors

Service calls, panel upgrades and larger project bids move at different speeds. We separate them in the CRM so quick jobs get booked fast and bigger quotes get a follow-up plan instead of waiting on a callback.

Roofing and solar

Roofing and solar jobs are high-value and considered decisions, often involving more than one decision-maker. Estimate follow-up and a clear record of every open proposal matter more here than almost anywhere else.

Concrete contractors

Concrete work is often bid, scheduled around weather and sold on the estimate visit. We make sure every bid is tracked, followed up and visible in the weekly numbers, so the owner can see what is open and what closed.
