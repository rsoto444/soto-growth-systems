What a Business Growth Consultant Fixes: the 10 Growth Leaks

Growth usually stalls in the same ten places. Most businesses have several open at once.

From our own audit

When we audited our own booking flow, we found the calendar's default consent checkbox was forced as required. Anyone who skipped it got an error and couldn't book a strategy call. It's exactly the kind of silent leak we look for in client systems: nothing looks broken, but qualified people drop off at the last step.

See all 10 Growth Leaks™

  1. Lead source

    Lead sources are unclear, untracked, or over-concentrated in one channel.

  2. Lead capture

    Inquiries arrive through forms, calls, or messages that aren't consistently logged anywhere.

  3. Speed-to-lead

    New leads sit for hours before anyone reaches out.

  4. Follow-up

    Follow-up happens once, or not at all, instead of on a defined cadence.

  5. Appointments

    Scheduled calls and estimates are missed, rescheduled, or never confirmed.

  6. Sales process

    Every rep, or the owner, sells a different way with no repeatable structure.

  7. CRM discipline

    The CRM exists but the data in it can't be trusted or acted on.

  8. KPI visibility

    No one can say, without guessing, what's actually happening in the pipeline this week.

  9. Accountability

    No one owns follow-through on leads, tasks, or deals once they're assigned.

  10. Owner bottleneck

    Growth stalls whenever the owner isn't personally involved in every deal.

Growth Strategy Consultant vs. Marketing Agency vs. Strategy Firm

People searching for a growth consultant often mean one of three different things. Here is where Soto Growth Systems fits.

A marketing agency brings in more leads

Ads, SEO and content increase how many people find you. That helps only if the leads that arrive get answered, followed up and closed. If they do not, more marketing mostly buys more leaks. Soto Growth Systems does not run ads or SEO; that work is handled by our partner Provo SEO Pros when it is needed.

A large strategy consulting firm plans for corporations

Large firms build strategy for enterprise companies with internal teams to carry it out. An owner-led business usually needs the opposite: a clear diagnosis, a practical design and help installing it, sized for a team where the owner still touches most deals.

A growth consultant fixes the system between the lead and the sale

That is what Soto Growth Systems does. We find where revenue leaks between first contact and closed deal, design the fix, and install it in your CRM, follow-up, sales process, KPIs and accountability, so growth stops depending on the owner.
