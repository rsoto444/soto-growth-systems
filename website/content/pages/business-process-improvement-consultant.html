How Operational Efficiency Consulting Works Here

  1. Map it as it really runs

    We walk through the process with the people who do it, not the version in anyone's head.

  2. Find where it breaks

  3. Design the fix

At Soto Growth Systems that ends with written SOPs, workflows and automation where they help, and a named owner for every process.

The Professional Growth Leak Assessment starts at $997. A Growth OS Blueprint with up to 10 custom SOPs starts at $7,500. Guided Implementation starts at $18,000 plus $4,500 a month, and Managed Implementation starts at $35,000 plus $8,500 a month. Scope and price are confirmed in writing first.

Do you give accounting, legal or HR advice?

No. We improve how finance, HR and admin work gets done: the steps, the handoffs, the SOPs and who owns them. Decisions on accounting, legal and HR matters stay with you and your professional advisers.

Is this business process management consulting?

It overlaps. Business process management often means software and ongoing process governance in larger companies. We focus on owner-led businesses: fixing the processes that slow the team down and making sure they keep running without the owner.

How long does process improvement take?

A Growth OS Blueprint is delivered in 4 to 6 weeks. Guided Implementation runs on a 90-day core, and Managed Implementation has a 6-month minimum. The timeline for your processes is confirmed in scope.

No. We guarantee a defined process, documented deliverables, agreed quality checks and clear reporting on what is done and what is open. Results depend on your team, adoption and the factors outside our control.
