Free Small Business Advice vs. a Paid Consultant

Both have a place. Here is how to tell which one you need right now.

When free help is the right call

If you are starting out, writing a business plan, looking for funding or have not made steady sales yet, start with your local Small Business Development Center. They offer no-cost advising in every state. Find yours on the SBA resource partners page.

When a paid consultant is the right call

If the business already sells steadily, earns around $500,000 or more a year and has outgrown the way it is run, advice alone rarely fixes it. You need someone to design the system, write it down and help install it. That is the work Soto Growth Systems does.
