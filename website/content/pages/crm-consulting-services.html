Signs You Need a CRM System Consultant, Not More Software

A CRM rarely fails because of the software. It fails because it was never set up around the way leads really move.

From our own audit

When we audited our own booking flow, we found the calendar's default consent checkbox was forced as required. Anyone who skipped it got an error and couldn't book a strategy call. It's exactly the kind of silent leak we look for in client systems: nothing looks broken, but qualified people drop off at the last step.

See the six warning signs
  • Leads arrive by form, call and message, and not all of them land in the CRM
  • Pipeline stages do not match what is actually happening with deals
  • Duplicate contacts, empty fields and old deals nobody closed out
  • Follow-up depends on someone remembering, not on the system
  • The owner cannot see close rate or open pipeline without asking around
  • The team keeps its own spreadsheets because it does not trust the CRM

How We Set Up GoHighLevel: Our Own Lead System

Our own website runs on the same kind of setup we build for clients.

What happens when someone sends us an enquiry

  1. It lands in the CRM

    The website form sends the enquiry straight into GoHighLevel and creates or updates the contact, with the business name in its own field.

  2. It is tagged

    Every website enquiry gets a tag, and a second tag records whether the person agreed to text messages.

  3. It becomes a deal

    An opportunity is created in the pipeline at the first stage, so it is visible until someone moves it forward.

  4. Someone is told

    An email alert goes out the moment it arrives, so nothing waits for someone to check the CRM.

  5. They can book straight away

    The thank-you page shows the strategy call calendar, so a ready buyer can book in the same minute.
